AI Use Disclosure Statement

Artificial intelligence (AI) tools were used as supplementary resources in the completion of this activity. AI was utilized to assist in gathering, organizing, and consolidating the information needed for the selected chemical compounds. This included obtaining initial information regarding their chemical structures, physical and chemical properties, characteristics, applications, notable features, potential risks, and other relevant information required for the completion of the activity.

AI tools were also used to develop a better understanding of the selected compounds and the concepts associated with them. AI-generated explanations were consulted to clarify technical concepts and provide simpler interpretations of topics related to molecular structures, functional groups, chemical properties, reactivity, intermolecular interactions, and other relevant chemical principles. This assistance was used primarily as a learning aid to improve understanding of the compounds and to determine which information was relevant and appropriate to include in the final output.

In addition, AI was used to assist in organizing ideas and improving the clarity, conciseness, and overall presentation of certain portions of the work. Suggestions generated by AI were reviewed and modified as necessary to ensure that they were consistent with the objectives and requirements of the activity. AI-generated information was not accepted without consideration; the information was evaluated based on its relevance and my understanding of the subject matter before being incorporated into the final output.

The use of AI in this activity was therefore limited to research assistance, conceptual clarification, information organization, and language refinement. I remained responsible for reviewing, interpreting, selecting, and presenting the information included in the submitted work. AI was used as a supplementary learning and writing tool and did not replace my own analysis, judgment, or understanding of the subject matter. The final output was reviewed prior to submission to ensure that the content was relevant, accurate to the best of my knowledge, and aligned with the requirements of the activity.
